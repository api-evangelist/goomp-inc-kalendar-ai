Cockpit AI is an AI-worker platform for outbound sales, marketing and revenue operations. Its
named workers ("Vinay" for outbound, "Amara") research prospects, draft outreach and proposal
documents in the customer's voice, send from the customer's own email and LinkedIn accounts,
follow up across channels, and book meetings on the customer's calendar with a human approval
step before anything ships. The company previously operated as Kalendar AI on kalendar.ai; that
domain now 301-redirects site-wide to oncockpit.ai.

## What an agent can and cannot do here

- There is **no public developer API**. No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection,
  SDK, CLI or webhook catalog is published on any reachable host.
- There is **no MCP server** and **no A2A agent card**.
- The API host `https://api.oncockpit.ai` is live and self-identifies
  (`{"name":"Cockpit AI API","status":"active"}`) but serves no unauthenticated reference. Its
  robots.txt disallows all crawling.
- The one machine-readable artifact the company publishes is a legacy ChatGPT plugin manifest,
  still branded "KalendarAI", at `https://api.oncockpit.ai/.well-known/ai-plugin.json`. It
  declares OAuth with the scopes `read write` and names an OpenAPI at
  `https://kalendar.ai/openapi.yaml` — a URL that no longer serves a spec.
- Pricing is not published. The only conversion path is a demo booking.

## Note on crawling

disallows ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot,
Applebot-Extended and meta-externalagent. api.oncockpit.ai disallows all user agents.
